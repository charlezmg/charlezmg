Workflow

Keeping each account organized

How the work was tracked

  • Each client brand had its own Trello board
  • Pin titles and tasks were logged on individual cards
  • Due dates and upcoming publishing needs were tracked
  • Revisions and client communication stayed with the relevant task

Asset management

Keeping client assets separated and easy to find

  • Structured Google Drive and Dropbox folders for each account
  • Source files, drafts, and final exports kept separate
  • Relevant file links added to Trello when needed
  • Clear separation between client accounts and their assets

Output documentation

Keeping a record of published work

  • Completed pins logged in Google Sheets
  • Canva template links recorded
  • Source blog URLs recorded
  • Published URLs recorded
  • Records used for tracking and invoicing documentation
